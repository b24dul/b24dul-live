Chapter 1 progress

## During Pipework
> When Jason goes to collect Mandy's rent
- Pick up the Macizo Cigars [missable]
  Fix Mandy's sink and watch for her to throw a box outside.
  Walk onto the front lawn and use the pickup prompt on the cigar box.
  Deadline: before leaving Mandy's property

## During Get Shirley's Rum
> When Shirley calls Jason over near his house around the time of Pipework
- Bring Shirley her rum [missable]
  Guides disagree on whether Shirley first calls before or after Pipework. Check near Jason's house on both trips.
  Speak to Shirley at her gate and agree to get her rum.
  Go to the marked Three Sheets Liquor shop south of Jason's house.
  Face the clerk, hold L2 and press X to ask for the Salty Squid rum.
  Return to Shirley and hand her the bottle.
  Deadline: before starting Tourist Trap

## During Steal the Scooter
> When Shirley calls Jason over again, roughly one in-game day after the rum errand
- Bring Shirley the mobility scooter [missable]
  Speak to Shirley and accept the favor.
  Cross the water behind her house or follow the road around to Peggy's place.
  Find the scooter in Peggy's driveway beside the stairs and get on.
  Drive back to Shirley and park inside her porch when she opens the door.
  Deadline: before starting Tourist Trap

## During Island Dreams
> When Kenton sends Jason out with the bachelor party
- Recover treasure from the shipwreck
  Follow the divers through the underwater trench to the wreck.
  Search the seabed outside the hull for a gold chest icon.
  Use the interact prompt at the icon to collect the treasure.

## After Island Dreams
> When Jason returns to free roam after Kenton's first dive
- Start Kenton's treasure dives
  Look for Kenton's next dive marker and head to the marked search area.
  Dive and look for the treasure pickup prompt on the seabed.
  Deadline: before Tourist Trap if you want to dive before the Chapter 2 pause

## During Buy Lucia a Necklace

- Buy Lucia's engraved necklace [missable]
  Take the necklace errand to The Siren's Chest beside the barbershop in southern Key Lento.
  Speak to the clerk and buy the custom gold chain for $475.

## After Buck Me

- Move money into Family Savings
  Open BuckMe on the phone and choose Family Savings.
  Transfer up to $5,000 to carry it into Chapter 2. Cash on hand, checking and the house stash are lost.

## After Down to Earth
> When Jason is back in free roam after the seaplane job
- Meditate with Lucia [missable]
  Check the kitchen at Jason's house in the morning and talk to Lucia. Check again another morning if she doesn't bring up meditation.
  Follow her to the balcony with the workout equipment.
  Face Lucia, hold L2 and choose Accept.
  Unconfirmed: the session adds one to the meditation counter, but its effect on the Positive Mental Attitude trophy is unclear.
  Deadline: before starting Tourist Trap
- Sell goods at Donnie's drop-off
  Take any goods you want to sell to Donnie's marked drop-off and use the sale prompt.
  Guides disagree on when he stops buying. Selling before Clear Skies is the safer timing.
  Deadline: aim to sell before starting Clear Skies

## During Find the Lost Drugs
> When the phone call adds four swamp search zones to the map
- Recover the lost swamp packages [missable]
  Guides disagree on the unlock: check for the call after Buck Me and again after Down to Earth.
  In the southern zone, shoot the package down from the tree by the beach and check the water at the end of the dock.
  In the next zone north, check the burned houses on both shores and the blue kiddie pool at the southwest edge.
  In the third zone, look beside the alligators. In the northernmost zone, take the half brick from inside the shed.
  Take the haul to Brian's lockup or Donnie's drop-off if it is still open.
  Deadline: before starting Tourist Trap

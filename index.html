Welcome to the stream

Enter your name to join. Only the site administrator can see your real name; other viewers see a random nickname.

The site also records visit and activity counts, your device/browser type and the website you came from (if available). The administrator can view these records. Your chat nickname and messages are visible to other visitors.

Chat rule: keep it anonymous. Don't ask anyone for their name, age, gender, location or socials, and don't share your own.

What this site records, in plain English

No hidden tracking and no "accept cookies" trick. Here is everything, and only B24DUL can see it:

  • The name you type here (other viewers never see it), General usage and analytics statistics
  • Anonymous visitor counts through GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across other sites

That's it. No adverts, no selling or sharing your info, and no tracking on other websites. This site saves your random chat name and your Sudoku and 2048 progress on your device so they're still there next time. If you make a game leaderboard, only your random chat name is shown. The YouTube video player is run by YouTube, which may use its own cookies.

By clicking Enter site you're OK with the above. You can at any time.
